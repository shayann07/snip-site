Local-First Guarantee

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026 · Effective Date: September 16, 2026

The Core Promise: Snip is built from the ground up as a 100% local-first, offline application. There is no network access code, no cloud synchronization, no user account system, no telemetry, and zero third-party analytics trackers. Your audio tracks, recordings, and contacts never leave your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike conventional media tools, Snip does not collect, process, or transmit personal data to external servers:

  • No Internet Permission: The application does not declare the Android android.permission.INTERNET permission. It is technically impossible for the application to initiate HTTP requests or transmit data over the internet.
  • No User Identification: We do not ask for, generate, or store identifiers such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, advertising IDs, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not embed Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, or any other tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No Crash Telemetry: Crash logs and diagnostic data stay entirely on your device's local system log buffer (Logcat). We receive zero automated crash reports.

2. Android Permissions & Why We Need Them

Snip requests only the minimum permissions required to inspect, edit, encode, and assign audio files directly on your Android device:

A. Audio Storage (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Allows Snip to scan your device's MediaStore index so you can browse and select songs, voice notes, and installed ringtones to trim. Usage is read-only for source files; Snip never modifies, deletes, or overwrites your original audio tracks.

B. Modifying System Audio Settings (WRITE_SETTINGS)

Required exclusively when you instruct Snip to set an exported audio clip as your default Phone Ringtone, Notification Sound, or Alarm. Android requires explicit system permission to alter system audio URI preferences.

C. Contacts (READ_CONTACTS & WRITE_CONTACTS)

Required exclusively if you choose the "One Contact Only" ringtone feature. This allows you to pick a contact from your address book and attach a specific ringtone URI to their contact record. Within the app, contact phone numbers are automatically masked (e.g. 98••• 41220) for shoulder-surfing privacy. Contact data is read in-memory and is never persisted into any external database.

D. Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used only when you actively navigate to the built-in Sound Recorder tab and tap "Start Recording". Captured audio is encoded directly to your device's local storage under Music/Snip/recordings.

E. Foreground Services & Notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE & POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used solely to keep the CPU active during multi-second native audio encoding (LAME MP3 / libvorbis OGG) and live microphone recording, preventing the operating system from terminating the process mid-operation. A persistent notification is shown only while an active job is running.

3. Where Data is Stored

All clips exported by Snip are saved to your local storage under the standard public directory:

/storage/emulated/0/Music/Snip/

Metadata regarding your saved clips (clip title, custom in/out boundaries, duration, and creation timestamp) is stored in a private local SQLite database powered by SQLDelight on your device. When you uninstall Snip or clear application storage, the internal database is removed. Exported audio files in your Music folder remain accessible to you unless explicitly deleted.

4. Third-Party Code & Native Libraries

Snip bundles native C audio libraries compiled with the Android NDK for high-performance audio synthesis and encoding:

  • LAME 3.100 (MP3 encoder) — licensed under GNU LGPL v2.1.
  • libogg 1.3.5 & libvorbis 1.3.7 (OGG Vorbis encoder) — licensed under the Xiph BSD-style license.

These libraries run entirely offline in local native process memory and contain zero network capabilities.

5. Children's Privacy

Snip is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, because it has no means of sending information anywhere.

6. Changes to this Policy

Any updates to this Privacy Policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Because Snip has no network connection, no in-app telemetry will ever notify us of your identity.
