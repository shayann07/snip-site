04

What it doesn’t do

  • No account.Open it and start trimming. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No internet permission.Snip doesn’t ask Android for network access, so it can’t send anything anywhere.
  • No analytics, no ads, no crash reporter.Nothing in the app watches what you do.
  • It never edits your original file.Snip reads the track and writes a new clip beside it.
Clips are saved to /Music/Snip and stay there if you uninstall. Read the privacy policy
